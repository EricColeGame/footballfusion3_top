## Pro Tips to Dominate on Day One

Whether you entered the arena immediately following the **Football Fusion 3 release date** or are just stepping onto the virtual turf, adopting proven habits will rapidly improve your win rate and stat ledger.

### 1. Master Risk vs. Reward Passing
Throwing into double coverage severely harms your QBR metric. Check down to running backs or slot receivers running crossing routes if the deep safety over-commits to deep posts.

### 2. Protect Your Target Efficiency
Because a 50% catch-to-target ratio is mandatory for elite overall progression, avoid calling for passes when tightly bracketed. Only call for the ball when you have established clear outside leverage or inside positioning on slant routes.

### 3. Tackle in Tandem
Attempting solo dive tackles against shifty ball-carriers can lead to missed tackles that drag down your tackle-per-game average. Funnel runners toward the sideline or let your defensive interior initiate the wrap-up before securing the stop.
