To earn the prestigious 99 rating naturally, you must sustain the following baseline statistics across your qualified matches:

| Metric / Stat Goal | Required Minimum Threshold | Primary Position Impacted | Tips to Achieve |

| **Tackles Per Game (TPG)** | 7 TPG | Linebacker / Safety | Maintain proper gap discipline and tackle angles. |

### Quarterback (QB) Strategy
* **Read Coverages Pre-Snap:** Identify single-high vs. two-high safety alignments to spot open deep zones.
* **Lead Passes Accurately:** Use bullet passes for tight windows over the middle and lob passes to lead receivers on fade routes toward the sideline.

### Wide Receiver (WR) Strategy
* **Vary Your Route Tree:** Mix quick ins, outs, hitches, and deep post routes to keep opposing cornerbacks guessing.
* **High-Point the Football:** Jump into incoming passes instead of letting the football drop passively into your chest.

### Defensive Back (DB) Strategy
* **Maintain Inside Leverage:** Force receivers toward the boundary where the sideline acts as an extra defender.
* **Bait Quarterbacks:** Play underneath zone coverage and break toward the ball the moment the throw is initiated to secure pick-sixes.
