### Step 1: Set Up Your Free Agent Profile
Once you join a league server, navigate to the `#roles` or `#free-agents` channel. Select your primary positions (such as QB, WR, CB, or MLB) and state your availability timezone.

### Step 2: Attend Scouting Combines
Before season drafts, commissioners host combine sessions in private servers. Scouts evaluate your:
* Catch radius and timing on deep routes
* Quarterback bullet pass precision and release timing
* Defensive coverage discipline and tackle angles

### Step 3: Compile a Short Highlight Reel
Franchise owners review clip channels to make draft decisions. Record short clips of diving catches, red-zone stops, and game-winning drives to share directly in player showcase channels.

| Position | Primary Evaluation Metric | Key Skill to Highlight in Tryouts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Cornerback (CB)** | Interceptions / Pass Deflections | Hip-swivel recovery, closing speed, zone discipline |
| **Linebacker / Safety** | Tackle Efficiency / Blitz Pressure | Gap filling, open-field tackling, ball strip timing |

## Server Rules, Safety, and Fair Play

To maintain a healthy competitive environment, every major **Football Fusion 3 discord** enforces strict sportsmanship and safety policies. Familiarizing yourself with these standard guidelines protects your roster spot and prevents server bans.

* **Anti-Toxicity & Sportsmanship:** Trash talk during game days is common, but personal harassment, hate speech, and server raiding result in instant permanent bans.
* **Fair Play & Anti-Exploit Policies:** Utilizing third-party macros, speed injection scripts, or lag switches will get you blacklisted across affiliated league federations.
* **Scheduling Commitments:** If your team schedules a game, failing to show up without 24 hours notice typically leads to a forfeit and free agent penalty points.
* **Prize Payout Verification:** High-tier leagues offering Robux rewards (often between 5,000 and 15,000+ Robux) or real-world cash prizes require verified Roblox group payouts or legitimate payment methods to prevent scam tournaments.
