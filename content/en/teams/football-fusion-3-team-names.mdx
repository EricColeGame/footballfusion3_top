## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do Football Fusion 3 team names differ from real NFL franchises?
The developers at XSTNS Games use custom team identities—such as the New York Greats or Dallas Outlaws—to avoid trademark infringement with the National Football League (NFL) while preserving familiar city locations and color schemes.

### How do I change my team jersey and number in Football Fusion 3?
You can change your uniform appearance by acquiring the Customization gamepass for 290 Robux. This grants access to the uniform editor where you can change nameplates, numbers, and gear accessories to match your selected club from the official **Football Fusion 3 team names** list.

### Can I pick which team I play for in every game?
Yes. If you own the Auto Captain gamepass (600 Robux), you gain pregame privileges that allow you to select your preferred team, choose jersey sides (home or away), draft lobby players, and call the coin toss.

### What happens when a team builds a huge lead in Football Fusion?
The game features an automated mercy rule designed to keep matches competitive. If a team leads by 46 to 48 points (or 40 points with unbalanced rosters), the match automatically concludes to prevent lopsided server stalling.
